# yw04-regex: why the entry points take a ReScratch (G33's shape at the engine). One dfa_find_end of
# a?{25}a{25} over 25 a's, release tier, in the container, mean of >= 300 ms of calls in one function:
#   each call's tables allocated in the caller's (session-long) region:   58,832 ns a call
#   the same call inside a per-iteration `region r { }`:                  17,498 ns a call
# C mallocs and frees the DFA's arena per scan (dfa.c:536-568, arena_free_all at return); a wolf
# allocation lives to its region's end, so per-call storage grows the region (page faults, fresh
# memory) and is never returned in an editor whose loop region is the session's. The port's DFA and
# Pike VM therefore run on reusable storage: re_search_ws / re_test_ws / re_search_back_ws /
# re_search_any_ws take the caller's ReScratch and allocate nothing once its tables have grown;
# re_search and friends make a scratch per call. perf-re times the _ws entry points.
