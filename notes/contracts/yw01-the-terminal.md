## Prediction (write before the first change)
The mode int reaches `ICANON`/`ECHO`/`ISIG`/`IXON`/`IEXTEN`/`VMIN`/`VTIME` and not `ICRNL`/`OPOST`: the Enter witness will show LF, the `\n` witness will show CRLF, and both are filed before the first PTY golden is attempted (sagitta's goldens assume both bits cleared, so campaign-01's `input_keys_*` and every paint case will be red until the widening lands or the maintainer rules an interim). The lone-ESC deadline and the probe deadline need a wait with a timeout, which `os_signal_wait_fd` lacks (G2): the port polls `VTIME`-timed reads (`Mode.time` = 1, 100 ms granularity) until R2 lands, and the 25 ms ESC timeout becomes 100 ms in the `nokitty` profile only — a measured, documented divergence in timing, not in bytes; if a golden's bytes depend on it, STOP. Cold start of the port will be within 2x of C's 648 us (the stub is already a wolf process start); keypress → paint for `demo_paint`'s `damage` scene within 2x. Nothing in campaign 01 allocates per keystroke after warm-up except `Key.text`, which the port keeps as a reused `List[byte]`.
